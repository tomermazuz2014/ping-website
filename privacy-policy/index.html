8A. Third-party services and technologies in the apps

The Ping apps (Ping for customers and Ping for business) and "Ping in the browser" use the following services:

  • Supabase: database hosting, user authentication (email and password, Google, Apple, and SMS verification), file storage (such as business photos) and server functions. Account data, appointments, messages and reviews are stored there.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): push notifications. A device token linked to the account is stored and is removed when the account is deleted.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google): usage measurement. Screens viewed and in-app actions (for example selecting a category, viewing a business, starting and completing a booking), the app version, operating system and language, and the Ping account identifier used as the analytics user ID are collected. Ping does not send your name, phone number or email to analytics, and does not send precise location. Analytics data is not automatically deleted when the account is deleted (see below).
  • SMS delivery: verification codes and operational messages are sent through an external SMS provider, which receives the phone number and message content.
  • Google and Apple: sign-in with a Google or Apple account, at your choice. Ping receives the details needed to identify the account (such as email and name).
  • Address search (Photon): in the business app, the address text typed in search is sent to an external address search service (photon.komoot.io).
  • Location: in the customer app, if you grant location permission, it is used to calculate distance from businesses. The permission is optional and can be revoked in the device settings.
  • Contacts: in the business app, if you choose to import clients from your contacts, the contacts you select are added to the business's client list. The permission is optional.

8B. Account deletion and retained data

How to delete: a user of Ping for customers deletes their own account from the Profile tab, "Delete account", in the app or in "Ping in the browser": type DELETE and verify identity (password, or a fresh Google/Apple sign-in within 10 minutes). Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Detailed instructions: account deletion page.

What is deleted: the account and profile, favorite businesses, waitlist entries, notification preferences, notification devices, conversations and messages, reviews and review reports you submitted, and a Google Calendar connection set up in the staff member's name, including the tokens stored on our servers (see section 9). A business-level calendar connection remains as long as the business exists. Future active appointments are cancelled.

What remains: past appointment records remain with the business for its own history, without a link to the deleted account. Details a business saved about its own customer (such as name and phone in the customer record) are held by the business. Administrative and security records (event log) are kept without a link to the account. Firebase Analytics data already collected is retained by Google under its retention settings and is not deleted automatically; you may request its deletion by email. Periodic backups may contain copies until they are replaced.

Business owners: an account on the team of an active business is deleted from Ping for business. A sole owner cannot delete the account while other customers have active future appointments at the business. When deletion proceeds, the business is hidden and removed from search and its phone and email are cleared. If the business has other owners, it continues to exist.

Request by email: anyone who cannot sign in may send a deletion request to tomermazuz2014@gmail.com. We will ask to verify your identity before deleting. Handling is manual with no guaranteed turnaround time.

9. Use of Google APIs and Google Calendar

Business owners may connect their Google Calendar to Ping. The connection is optional, is made with explicit consent on the Google permissions screen, and can be revoked at any time.

  • Scope requested: the only permission Ping requests is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events — access to calendar events.
  • How we use it: reading existing events in order to mark those hours as busy in the business's Ping calendar; creating and updating events for appointments booked through Ping; and deleting those events when an appointment is cancelled. Ping creates, updates and deletes only events that Ping itself created.
  • How we store it: Ping stores on its servers the Google event identifier, the event start and end times, the event title, together with a sync token and an OAuth refresh token used for ongoing access to the calendar. This information is retained for as long as the connection remains active.
  • Deletion: when the connection is disconnected — whether from the app settings or by revoking access in your Google account — Ping immediately stops accessing the calendar and deletes all Google Calendar data it holds: the refresh token, the sync token, and every stored event identifier, event time and event title. No Google Calendar data is retained after disconnection.
  • Ping does not sell information from Google Calendar, and does not transfer it to third parties other than as necessary to operate the Service, where required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • Ping does not use or transfer Google Calendar data for: targeted, personalised or retargeted advertising; sale to data brokers or information resellers; determining credit-worthiness or for lending purposes; or training generalised or non-personalised AI or machine learning models.
  • Humans do not read Google Calendar data, except with your explicit consent, for security purposes, where required by law, or in aggregated and anonymised form for internal operations.
  • Ping's use of information received from Google APIs, and the transfer of that information, adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The connection can be disconnected at any time from the app settings, or on the Google account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
